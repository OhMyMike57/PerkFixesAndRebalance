# Party leader perks

151 perks. Bannerlord 1.4.8 + War Sails 1.2.8, with Perk Fixes and Rebalance (all settings at default). Effect text is what the game shows with Perk Fixes and Rebalance installed.

A party leader effect applies to the party the hero leads. Companions leading clan parties use their own perks for their party. In an army, each party uses its own leader.

- **Skill**: skill level required. Perks at the same level of a skill come in pairs; you pick one. **Alternative** is the other one.
- **Other effect of this perk**: the effect you also get from the same perk, with the role it needs.
- Effects that Perk Fixes and Rebalance moves to another role are listed at the bottom instead of in the tables.

## Moved to another role by Perk Fixes and Rebalance

These were party leader effects in vanilla.

- **Sledges** (Medicine 75): +15 hit points to mounts in your party. Now **Surgeon**.
- **Fortitude Tonic** (Medicine 225): +10 hit points to other heroes in your party. Now **Surgeon**.
- **Gilded Purse** (Boatswain 100): +25% to chance of capturing ships after battle. Now **First mate**.
- **Popular Captain** (Boatswain 175): +5% combat deck size for the ship under the character's command. Now **First mate**.
- **Port Authority** (Boatswain 200): +1 ship to command limit in naval battles. Now **First mate**.
- **Blessings Of The Sea** (Boatswain 200): +1 ship to command limit in naval battles. Now **First mate**.
- **Shipwright's Hand** (Boatswain 225): Discarded ships repair party ships. Now **First mate**.
- **Merchant Fleet** (Boatswain 250): +1 ship to command limit in naval battles. Now **First mate**.
- **Unflinching** (Shipmaster 175): +100% to disembarkation speed. Now **Navigator**.
- **Shore Master** (Shipmaster 175): -50% to ship recall time. Now **Navigator**.
- **Fleet Commander** (Shipmaster 200): -30% to fleet size penalty for campaign map movement. Now **Navigator**.
- **Fleet Commander** (Shipmaster 200): -20% to skeleton crew requirement for any ship in party. Now **Navigator**.
- **Stormrider** (Shipmaster 225): +1 ship to command limit in naval battles. Now **Navigator**.
- **Master and commander** (Shipmaster 225): +1 ship to command limit in naval battles. Now **Navigator**.
