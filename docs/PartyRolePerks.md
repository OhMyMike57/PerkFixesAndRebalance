# Party role perks

121 perks. Bannerlord 1.4.8 + War Sails 1.2.8, with Perk Fixes and Rebalance (all settings at default). Effect text is what the game shows with Perk Fixes and Rebalance installed.

A party role effect applies to the party of the hero assigned to that role in the clan screen: scout, quartermaster, surgeon, engineer, and with War Sails first mate and navigator.

- If a role is empty, or the assigned hero isn't in the party (for example wounded and left in a settlement), the party leader's perks count for that role.
- With Perk Fixes and Rebalance's experimental **Role perks from party leader or role hero** option (off by default), a role effect applies if either the role hero or the party leader has the perk, counted once. So the leader and a companion can take opposite perks of a pair and get both. Effects that scale with skill (Price of Loyalty, Uncanny Insight, Masterwork, Minister of Health, Doctor's Oath) use the higher skill of the two. AI parties get this too.
- Each role lists the perks of its own skill first, then role effects from other skills' perks.
- **Skill**: skill level required. Perks at the same level of a skill come in pairs; you pick one. **Alternative** is the other one.
- **Other effect of this perk**: the effect you also get from the same perk, with the role it needs.
