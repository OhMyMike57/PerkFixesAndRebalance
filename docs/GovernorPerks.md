# Governor perks

125 perks. Bannerlord 1.4.8 + War Sails 1.2.8, with Perk Fixes and Rebalance (all settings at default). Effect text is what the game shows with Perk Fixes and Rebalance installed.

A governor effect applies to the town or castle the hero governs, and to villages bound to it.

- **Skill**: skill level required. Perks at the same level of a skill come in pairs; you pick one. **Alternative** is the other one.
- **Other effect of this perk**: the effect you also get from the same perk, with the role it needs.
