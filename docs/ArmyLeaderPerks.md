# Army leader perks

10 perks. Bannerlord 1.4.8 + War Sails 1.2.8, with Perk Fixes and Rebalance (all settings at default). Effect text is what the game shows with Perk Fixes and Rebalance installed.

An army leader effect applies when the hero leads an army, usually to every party in it.

- **Skill**: skill level required. Perks at the same level of a skill come in pairs; you pick one. **Alternative** is the other one.
- **Other effect of this perk**: the effect you also get from the same perk, with the role it needs.
